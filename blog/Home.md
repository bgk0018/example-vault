# Welcome to the Garden

This is a digital garden — a public workspace where ideas grow over time. Unlike a traditional blog, notes here are published at varying stages of maturity, interlinked, and revisited as understanding deepens.

## How to Navigate

- **[[Articles]]** — Finished pieces, polished and complete
- **[[Maps of Content]]** — Topic indexes that collect related notes
- **[[Highlights]]** — Curated quotes and passages from books and articles

## What's Growing

Notes in this garden range from seedlings (🌱) to evergreen (🌳). You'll find ideas at every stage of development — that's intentional. Learning in public means sharing the thinking, not just the conclusions.
