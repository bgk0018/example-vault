# Periodization

Periodization is the systematic planning of training into distinct phases (mesocycles), each with a specific physiological focus. The classic model moves through phases of increasing intensity and decreasing volume:

1. **Hypertrophy phase** — high volume, moderate intensity (build muscle mass)
2. **Strength phase** — moderate volume, high intensity (convert mass to force)
3. **Power/peaking phase** — low volume, very high intensity (sharpen for competition)
4. **Recovery/deload** — reduced load to allow supercompensation

More modern approaches include undulating periodization (varying volume and intensity within a single week) and block periodization (concentrating one quality per 2-4 week block).

The key insight is that you cannot maximize all physical qualities simultaneously. Attempting to train everything at once leads to a "mixed training effect" where nothing improves optimally.
