# Protein Timing

The "anabolic window" — the idea that you must consume protein within 30-60 minutes of training to maximize muscle growth — is far less important than total daily protein intake. Research consistently shows that hitting 1.6-2.2g of protein per kilogram of body weight per day matters more than when you eat it.

That said, distributing protein intake evenly across 3-4 meals (rather than consuming most of it in one sitting) does appear to optimize muscle protein synthesis rates. The body can only utilize roughly 0.4-0.55g/kg per meal for muscle building; excess is oxidized for energy.

For practical purposes:
- **Total daily protein** → most important variable
- **Per-meal distribution** → moderately important
- **Post-workout timing** → least important (convenient, but not critical)
