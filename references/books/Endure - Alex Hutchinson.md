# Endure

>[!Summary]
> Alex Hutchinson explores the science of endurance — the surprising factors that determine our physical limits. Drawing on cutting-edge research and gripping stories of athletes pushing beyond what was thought possible, he reveals that the mind, not the body, is often the ultimate barrier.

> [!Done] I Learned
> The central governor theory fundamentally changed how I think about fatigue. It's not just about muscles running out of fuel — the brain actively regulates output to protect the body. This has huge implications for training and performance.

## Notes

- The book challenges the traditional "catastrophe model" of fatigue
- Central governor theory (Tim Noakes) is the backbone — the brain as limiter
- Progressive overload works partly because it teaches the brain to tolerate more discomfort
- Heat, altitude, hydration, and perception of effort all interact in complex ways
