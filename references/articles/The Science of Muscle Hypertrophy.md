# The Science of Muscle Hypertrophy

>[!Summary]
> A comprehensive review of the mechanisms behind muscle growth, covering mechanical tension, metabolic stress, and muscle damage as the three primary drivers. Discusses the role of progressive overload, volume, and frequency in optimizing hypertrophy.

## Key Points

- Mechanical tension is the primary driver of hypertrophy
- Progressive overload is necessary but insufficient alone — recovery and nutrition matter equally
- Training volume (sets × reps × load) is a stronger predictor of growth than any single variable
- Protein timing matters less than total daily protein intake
- Sleep deprivation can reduce muscle protein synthesis by up to 18%
