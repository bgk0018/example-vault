I was thinking about periodization today and realized that most beginner training programs don't use any periodization at all — they're just linear progressions that eventually stall.

What if the reason beginners plateau isn't lack of effort but lack of variation? The body adapts to a stimulus, and without changing the stimulus (volume, intensity, exercise selection), progress stalls.

But traditional periodization models are designed for competitive athletes with specific peak dates. Beginners don't have competitions. So what would "periodization for the general population" look like?

Maybe something like:
- 4 weeks of higher volume / moderate intensity
- 2 weeks of higher intensity / lower volume  
- 1 week deload
- Repeat
