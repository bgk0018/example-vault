## Gardening: Endure Plant Session
**Session type:** Plant
**Source:** [[Endure - Alex Hutchinson]]

Spent 30 minutes processing highlights from *Endure*. The central governor theory was the standout concept — the idea that the brain limits performance to protect the body, and that this limit can be trained.

Had a good discussion about how progressive overload works on two fronts: physiological (muscle adaptation) and neurological (the brain learning to tolerate more). This dual nature wasn't something I'd considered before.
