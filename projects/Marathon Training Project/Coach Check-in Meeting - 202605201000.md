# Coach Check-in Meeting - 202605201000

---

## Goals / Agenda
1. Review baseline fitness assessment results
2. Discuss heart rate zone training approach (MAF method vs. polarized)
3. Address previous knee injury — what mileage threshold to watch for

## Discussion Notes
- Dr. Chen reviewed my VO2max estimate from the Cooper test — approximately 42 ml/kg/min, which is "good" for age group but has room for improvement
- She recommended a polarized training approach: 80% of runs at Zone 2 (easy conversational pace), 20% at Zone 4-5 (tempo/intervals)
- This aligns with the [[Central Governor Theory - 202604201415|central governor theory]] — the brain needs to experience high-intensity efforts to recalibrate its limits, but the aerobic base is built at low intensity
- For the knee: keep weekly mileage below 35 miles for the first 4 weeks, then increase by 10% per week following [[Progressive Overload - 202604151030|progressive overload]] principles
- She recommended the book [[Why We Sleep - Matthew Walker|Why We Sleep]] for understanding recovery — said sleep is where the real adaptation happens
