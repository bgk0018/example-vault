# Objective

Complete a sub-4:00 marathon by training systematically over 16 weeks, using heart rate zone training and progressive overload principles to build endurance safely.

# Brainstorm

- Need to establish baseline fitness with a VO2max test before starting the plan
- Heart rate zone training (MAF method) for building aerobic base in weeks 1-8
- Transition to race-specific speed work in weeks 9-14
- Taper in weeks 15-16
- [[Progressive Overload - 202604151030|Progressive overload]] applies — increase weekly mileage by no more than 10% per week
- [[Sleep and Recovery - 202605011200|Sleep]] is critical during heavy training blocks — aim for 8+ hours
- Nutrition: need to dial in fueling strategy for long runs (gels, electrolytes)
- [[Central Governor Theory - 202604201415|Central governor]] insight — mental training matters as much as physical
- Should work with [[Dr. Sarah Chen]] on periodization structure
- Risk: previous knee issues may flare up above 40 miles/week

# Metrics

- Complete 16-week training plan with <2 missed sessions per week
- Hit target heart rate zones during 80% of training runs
- Complete a 20-mile long run at race pace before taper
- Race day: finish in under 4:00:00
