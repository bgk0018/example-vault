# Training Program Design Framework

**Thesis:** Most recreational athletes would benefit from a simple decision framework for choosing their training structure, rather than copying elite programs wholesale.

The core variables are:
- Training age (years of consistent training)
- Available days per week
- Primary goal (strength, endurance, body composition, general health)
- Recovery capacity (sleep, stress, nutrition quality)

I think there's a 2×2 matrix here: training age (novice vs. intermediate+) × goal type (performance vs. health). Each quadrant would have a different recommended approach to [[Periodization - 202605150900|periodization]] and volume.
